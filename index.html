03Mission Profile

international air and
space program 2027

07th - 13th NOVEMBER, 2027
HOUSTON, TEXAS

The IASP is a challenging 7-day educational program held in Houston, Texas. The students will work on a project that involves teamwork problem solving, and communication, among students and expert engineers of the aerospace industry to adapt & solve any unexpected problems.

Registrations are open for students worldwide. Spaces are limited. Before the program, all registered students will have access to a series of online courses in the aerospace field so no previous knowledge is required to apply to the program.
